How to locate your "Instagram" stream key?

Welcome to Qveedo, Let’s get started with a step-by-step guide to unlock your streaming potential.

Pre-Requirement

Before attempting to go live from your desktop, ensure your account meets the following conditions:


1. Professional Account: Your profile must be switched to either a Professional or Business account type.

2. Follower Threshold: Instagram typically requires accounts to have at least 1,000 followers to unlock Live Producer access.

Step 1: Login in your Instagram Account

Log into your target account on your computer . Click the Create / Add post button (represented by a plus symbol inside a square) located on the left-hand navigation menu.

Step 2: Guide to Live Broadcast

Select Live Video from the available creation options. Add your live broadcast details in the pop-up window:


Title: Enter a descriptive title so viewers know what your stream is about.
Audience: Select Public to broadcast openly, or choose Practice to test your connection privately without alerting followers

Click Next. Find A screen will appear displaying your unique Stream URL and Stream Key.

Step 3: Go Live!

Once you see your device on you computer, Click on "Go Live" and start streaming.
