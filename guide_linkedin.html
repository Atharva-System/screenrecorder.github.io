How to locate your "LinkedIn" stream key?

Welcome to Qveedo! Let’s get started with a step-by-step guide to unlock your live-streaming potential.

Pre-Requirement

Before attempting to go live, ensure your LinkedIn account or Page is eligible for LinkedIn Live access.

Step 1:

Log in to your LinkedIn account on your computer. Open LinkedIn Live Studio using this link https://www.linkedin.com/video/golive/later/

Step 2:

Schedule a live stream with all required details and click on “Schedule stream”

Step 3:

Navigate to Manage Stream and Refresh page , select your streaming and click on Prepare to go live.

Step 4:

Select your region from the Stream settings tab and click on the “Get URL” button. Then, copy the Stream URL and Stream Key.

If you don’t find your region in the dropdown list, choose the nearest location to your region.

Step 5:

Paste them into the respective fields in the Qveedo app. Tap Start Stream to continue.

Step 6:

Once your stream is connected and the preview is ready, return to LinkedIn Live Studio and click Go Live to start broadcasting.
